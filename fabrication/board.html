Material

Send this file to the laser as you saved it. The Universal lasers read only pure red (#FF0000) as a cut, at 0.001″. Recoloring, moving or rescaling a line, or switching an Illustrator file to CMYK, can make the laser skip it. A 0.001″ line looks faint or invisible in a browser; that's correct. To check it, zoom in to 400% or more, or use View › Outline in Illustrator.

Board art

Set up and export the board art the same way as the box wraps. The full walkthrough is in the Box art section of the box kit. The steps below cover what's different about boards.

1Save the template

Open the Board print tab and save the SVG. Open it in Illustrator, lock the template, and work on an Art layer underneath.

2Paint past every edge

3Respect the seams

4Think about where players sit

A board is read from every side of the table. Turn labels toward the player who uses them, or keep them symmetrical. Put the title on the edge nearest whoever sets up.

5Export and check

6Send it to Dr. Gotzler

Email the PDF to sgotzler@unc.edu, named teamname_board.pdf. It's printed at 100% on matte paper with the box wraps.

Assembly

Spraying isn't allowed at BeAM. Do every spray-adhesive and spray-finish step outdoors or in a well-ventilated room, over scrap cardboard.
