Fabrication

Laser cut layouts and print templates for your prototype

Two kits for building your physical prototype. Each one draws laser-ready cut files and print templates right in your browser, sized to the class chipboard. Pick your dimensions, download the SVGs, and take them to the cutter.

Wrapped bifold and quad-fold boards, mounted 4- and 6-panel boards, and flush-trimmed player boards. Lays out the chipboard for cutting, draws the print template for your art, and checks that the folded board fits in a class box.
